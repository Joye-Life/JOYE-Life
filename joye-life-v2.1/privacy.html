Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how JOYE (“JOYE,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you visit our website, join our waitlist, or use an early version of our products.

Launch note: This is a practical starter policy for the current beta. It should be reviewed by a qualified attorney before JOYE accepts payments, launches accounts, or processes sensitive financial or health data.

1. Information we collect

We may collect information you provide directly, including your email address, waitlist source, support messages, and information you intentionally enter into JOYE. The current dashboard prototype stores its demo data locally in your browser and does not send that dashboard data to our servers.

We may also receive basic technical information such as browser type, device type, referring page, approximate location derived from IP address, and usage events if analytics are enabled.

2. How we use information

We use information to operate and improve JOYE, manage the waitlist, communicate product updates, respond to support requests, protect the service, understand product usage, and comply with legal obligations.

3. Email communications

When you join the waitlist, we may send product updates and early-access invitations. You may unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in an email or by contacting us.

4. Service providers

We may use service providers for hosting, databases, email delivery, analytics, payments, and customer support. These providers process information on our behalf under their own terms and privacy practices. The initial production configuration is designed to use Vercel for hosting and Supabase for waitlist storage.

5. Cookies and local storage

JOYE may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, security, preferences, and analytics. The dashboard prototype uses browser local storage to preserve tasks, goals, career milestones, and paycheck-planning values on the device you use.

6. Data retention

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including legal, security, and operational needs. You may request deletion of your waitlist information.

7. Data security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, or to object to certain uses. To make a request, contact us using the address below. We may need to verify your identity.

9. Children

JOYE is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes

We may update this policy as JOYE develops. We will post the revised version and update the effective date.

11. Contact

Questions or privacy requests may be sent to privacy@joye.systems.
